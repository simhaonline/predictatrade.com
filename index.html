Context before the decision.

Predict-A-Trade brings multiple forms of market context together to make XAUUSD analysis easier to inspect, explain, and use responsibly.

Indicators

EMA and SMA trend references, MACD, RSI, ADX, ATR, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic, CCI, VWAP, and supporting volatility context.

Market structure

Swing highs and lows, Break of Structure, Change of Character, trend and range context, plus selected Fibonacci and pivot references.

Liquidity and zones

Liquidity pools, sweep and reclaim context, Fair Value Gaps, Order Blocks, and Breaker Blocks as part of the wider market picture.

Multi-timeframe alignment

M1 through H4 analysis with D1 and W1 context, helping shorter-term observations sit inside a broader directional view.

Sessions and regimes

Tokyo, London, overlap, and New York session context alongside trending, ranging, breakout, and changing-volatility conditions.

Explainable decision support

Evidence-led summaries that distinguish analytical probability from certainty and keep WAIT or NO-TRADE context visible when appropriate.
